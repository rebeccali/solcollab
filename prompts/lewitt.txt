# weight: 2
# Original instructions inspired by Sol LeWitt, rather than quotations of his work.

Draw a square using only short, disconnected lines.
Let five lines cross without meeting at the same point.
Fill one corner with circles that grow toward the center.
Divide the page into four rooms, each with a different kind of line.
Draw a grid, then let one line wander away.
Make a circle out of small triangles.
Draw twelve parallel lines and interrupt each one once.
Place a red shape inside a blue shape inside a yellow shape.
Draw an arc from each corner until the arcs meet.
Fill the page with lines that never touch.
Draw three squares that share one corner.
Make a border that gradually becomes the drawing.
